## Final prompt

Edit the attached reference image into ONE production-ready transparent-background island landmark asset for a bright Thai ocean-learning adventure website. The reference is the exact art-direction guide: cheerful saturated colors, soft rounded cartoon forms, polished mobile adventure game / gently 3D storybook illustration, warm sunlight, lush lime-green foliage, lavender-gray stone, cream carved arch, glowing books, turquoise water and small white flowers. Preserve that friendly tropical island-ruin character and coherent perspective; do NOT turn it into a formal library building, a triangular-roof facade, a castle, a cathedral or a flat line-art SVG. Recompose as a complete freestanding rocky island with a large central stone dimensional gateway centered exactly at 50% of the width. Natural rocky hill and lush plants surround the arch. On the left, a winding stone stairway and small ruined columns lead to a book on a stone plinth. On the right, a small rounded bright cyan waterfall flows down rocks into the sea. An open golden book sits at the summit; a modest carved book crest and two warm lanterns adorn the arch. Keep ornament restrained and playful, not baroque or photorealistic. Inside the arch show an opaque luminous blue/aqua magical passage receding into soft warm light, so no real background can show through the gateway. Keep a broad boat-accessible WATER opening at the center bottom: no deck, threshold, bridge, stairs, rock or pier crosses this opening. Remove the foreground wooden dock, boat, books in boat, all other boats, birds, people, mascots, foreground corner leaves, sky, clouds, distant islands, horizon and full-frame ocean background. Only the island itself, its gateway, plants, stone stairway, waterfall and a small amount of local water contact/reflection around the bases remain. Outside that silhouette is true transparent alpha, including the exterior water foreground, with clean gently fading water-contact edges, never a rectangular water patch. No text, logo, letters, interface or watermark. The whole landmark must fit fully inside a near-square 8:7 canvas with about 4% clear transparent padding on all sides. The island should occupy about 90% of the width; the front-facing arch water entry should be visible, unobstructed and centered. The outer silhouette is asymmetric and organic, with a larger rocky hill at upper right and stairs at lower left, but the actual gateway centerline remains exactly centered. This will be composited onto the website's live ocean; do not paint a scene background. Use the attached picture's bright, soft cartoon illustration style closely.
